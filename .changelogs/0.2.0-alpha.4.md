Fourth alpha of GTNH Planner: plan GT New Horizons factories in game, from NEI.

**This is an alpha.** Expect rough edges, and keep a backup of your world's planner saves (`gtnhplanner/plans.dat`, backed up automatically to `backups/` beside it). Bugs and ideas: the feedback key on the planner's top bar, or GTNH Planner's thread on the GT New Horizons Discord (the Discord key).

**Made for GTNH 2.9.** This build is made against GTNH 2.9.0-RC-2's mods (NEI 2.8.155, ModularUI2 2.3.92, GTNHLib 0.11.52, GT5-Unofficial 5.09.54, CropsNH 2.0.134). It is not for GTNH 2.8; stay on 0.2.0-alpha.1 there.

## New

- **Crops**: plan a crop from NEI's Crops page. The card is harvested by a Crop Manager or an Industrial Farm (pick it from the machine menu), with the tier chip stepping the manager's tier or the farm's seed bed, and the farm drawn as its whole structure.
- **Crop settings, worked out as the game does**: the crop's growth and gain, its water, fertilizer, sky and biome, and the farm's seed bed and units (accelerators, fertilizer, harvesting, biome cards, overclocks, sharing the farm's upgrade slots). The gear sheet shows the working: how well it is fed, how fast it grows, the harvest time, the drops, how many farms or managers the crops planted fill, the output and the power.
- **Crop power**: a crop card has a power row with its harvesters' draw (a farm draws in full however few crops it holds), counted in the overview's power.

## Changed

- A crop card's count is the crops planted ("crops planted" under it); the overview's machine list counts the farms and managers they fill.
- The gear sheet's formulas have more room: a long working wraps with its answer under it, and each setting's number is coloured.

## Fixed

- A card made from a recipe page NEI cannot look up again (CropsNH's Crops page) no longer crashes the planner.

## Install

1. Drop `gtnhplanner-0.2.0-alpha.4.jar` into your GTNH 2.9 instance's `mods` folder, replacing any older `gtnhplanner` jar (client only; servers don't need it).
2. Open your inventory: the GTNH Planner button is at the bottom left.

We're moving from alpha 5 to beta 1. This one is mostly about getting the numbers right and making everything faster. It should be solid enough for real use now. It's still a beta so the planner backs up your plans by itself.

Works on every GTNH 2.9 build from beta-1 to RC-2. Not on 2.8. Stay on 0.2.0-alpha.1 there.

Found a bug? Hit the red bug key in the planner or post in [our thread](https://discord.com/channels/181078474394566657/1531402304530682036) on the GTNH Discord.

> 📷 **IMAGE 1 – hero.** A mid-size GT line on the board, solved. Overview on the left and NEI on the right.

## New

- **Fluid Tank.** Fills or empties cells for free. Find it under Non-recipe → Other.

> 📷 **IMAGE 2 – Fluid Tank.** The tank card with a cell wired in and its fluid wired out to a machine.

- **Forced inputs and outputs** show right on the card. That's what the plan has to bring in or get rid of. Drag one off to make a drawer.

> 📷 **IMAGE 3 – Forced row.** Close-up of a card with a Forced row under one of its ports.

- **Split.** If extra output could go to more than one place you can split it between them. Or hit Auto.

> 📷 **IMAGE 4 – Split.** The overview's Forced section with its share bars and the Split and Auto keys.

- **The tour** now covers everything from NEI to placing machines in your world.

> 📷 **IMAGE 5 – Tour.** A tour note next to what it just did. A step out in the world would be best.

- **Bees.** Plan a bee from its NEI page in an Apiary, Magic Apiary or Industrial Apiary. Set its frames and upgrades. Say if it's jubilant to get its special products.
- **Share in chat.** Share → Share in chat drops your plan in the game chat. Anyone with the planner can click it open.
- **Pick the item.** An input that takes more than one item has a small orange mark. Scroll on it to swap Coal Dust for Carbon Dust and so on. New cards start on the item you favorited in NEI with Shift + F.
- The planner lets you know when an update is out.

> 📷 **IMAGE 6 (optional) – Update.** The new version box on the board.

## Better

- Smarter numbers. The planner makes things from raw materials when it can and only sells what nothing else can use.
- A machine nothing needs waits at 0 and asks how many you want.
- Much faster. Big plans that took minutes now take a second.
- No more hiccup after every change.
- Scrolling a number updates the plan as you scroll.
- Huge plans stay smooth.
- Dragging a wire lights up everywhere it can go.

> 📷 **IMAGE 7 – Wire in hand.** Mid-drag. The cards and ports that would take the wire glow green.

- Steam machines show how much steam they use.
- The Dangote Distillus shows its mode. In distillery mode you can set its height.

### In the world

> 📷 **IMAGE 8 – World.** Placed machines with labels on (Shift + I), wire labels and the card over the one you look at (Shift + U).

- Look at a machine to pop up its card (Shift + U). Turn on labels to see what each machine is (Shift + I).
- Wire labels are easier to read and don't pile up.
- Machines can be see-through, solid or hidden. Every world key can be changed in Controls.
- The settings show a preview of what each option does.

> 📷 **IMAGE 9 – Settings.** The gear's In the world section with its preview.

## Fixed

- Pasted plans get the right machines and tiers.
- Pasted plans bring their crop farms and keep their generator counts.
- You can stack placed machines on each other.
- Cards no longer stick out of the round minimap.
- Your rate unit and the overview's folded sections stay the way you left them.
- The tour leaves your GUI scale alone.
- Plans from the library open with all their cards. Even ones shared before this update.
- Changing a card's settings a few times in a row (like a boiler's fuel) no longer crashes the game.
- Crop cards show every drop. Lazulia gives its Lapis Lazuli again.
- The pin list and the world count the farms a crop needs instead of the crops planted.
- The Multi Smelter no longer says its coil is too cold.
- An HTGR from NEI is the reactor itself. TRISO fuel goes in and burned fuel comes out.
- Crash reports you send from the game show the crash again. They leave the chat out.
- The planner no longer gets blamed for NEI's own crashes on other screens.
- The board no longer comes up squashed after you add a recipe from NEI or come back from a recipe page.

## Install

Put `gtnhplanner-0.2.0-beta.1.jar` in your `mods` folder and delete the old one. You only need it on your client. The planner button is at the bottom left of your inventory.

Curious how it works inside? The [technical notes](0.2.0-beta.1-technical.md) go through the solver rewrite and the speed work with links into the code.

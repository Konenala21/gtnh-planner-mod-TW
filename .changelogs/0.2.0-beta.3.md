Beta 3. Works on every GTNH 2.9 build from beta-1 to RC-2. Back up your world before updating.

Machine counts work like real machines now. Set how many you have and the planner shows how hard each one runs and why. Extreme Entity Crushers are in too.

Found a bug? Hit the red bug key in the planner or post in [our thread](https://discord.com/channels/181078474394566657/1531402304530682036) on the GTNH Discord.

> 📷 **IMAGE 1 – hero.** A line of cards with Up to counts. A green FULL bar, a red BOTTLENECK and an amber STARVED with its short input lit.

## New

**Up to counts.** A count you set is now what you have. Your machines run as hard as the line lets them and never more. The bar along the bottom of the card says why: full, bottleneck, starved, clogged, unused or limited. The port holding a machine back lights up too. Pick Exactly to force a count like before. Counts you set before read as Up to.

> 📷 **IMAGE 2 – one card.** "×0.66 up to 1" under the machine, the STARVED bar along the bottom and the input it is short of lit up.

**Stuck loops.** Machines that starve or clog each other get a blinking warning and say so on their bars.

**Extreme Entity Crusher.** A card for every mob on NEI's Mob Drops page. Click its name to swap the mob. The gear sets your weapon, Looting and the rest.

**EU per item.** The rate key's new EU setting shows what each item or litre costs in power.

- Double-click a wire to pin a dot on it. Drag the dot and the wire follows.
- A Crop Farm card in Non-recipe. Pick the crop on the card.
- Crafting recipes can run on the Auto Workbench.
- Screenshots carry the plan inside them. Open plan file on the + tab opens them.

## Better

- Zoomed out cards show how hard each machine runs. Pick where they kick in under Settings.
- The overview lists every machine and every open input and output, even at zero. Drag its edge to resize it.
- The top bar always fits on one row. Invite people is first in the Share menu.
- Middle click clears any count or rate you set.
- Tier and amps chips are buttons again and you type amps and counts where they show.
- Deleting a drawer puts its rate back or wires its two machines together.
- A pencil key adds sticky notes, boxes and arrows.
- Steam machines in the overview show steam. Task spots copy their coordinates.
- Plan links and codes open in the rate unit they were shared in.

> 📷 **IMAGE 3 – zoomed out.** A few cards far out, each with its bar and its count.

## Fixed

- Signing in no longer hides the plans you made before.
- The Rock Breaker no longer asks you for lava.
- Tree Growth Simulator logs wire into the Industrial Coke Oven.
- Old Build plans and fluid tanks from plan links and codes work like they used to.

## Install

GTNH 2.9 only. Put `gtnhplanner-0.2.0-beta.3.jar` in your `mods` folder and delete the old one. It's client only.

Beta 3. Works on every GTNH 2.9 build from beta-1 to RC-2. Back up your world before updating.

Machine counts work like real machines now. Set how many you have and the planner shows how hard each one runs and why it can't run harder.

Found a bug? Hit the red bug key in the planner or post in [our thread](https://discord.com/channels/181078474394566657/1531402304530682036) on the GTNH Discord.

> 📷 **IMAGE 1 – hero.** A line of cards with Up to counts. A green FULL bar, a red BOTTLENECK and an amber STARVED with its short input lit.

## New

**Up to counts.** A count you set is now what you have. Your machines run as hard as the line lets them and never more. The bar along the bottom of the card says how hard and why: full, bottleneck, starved, clogged, unused or limited. The port holding a machine back lights up in the same colour. Pick Exactly on the count to force it like before. Every count you set before now reads as Up to.

> 📷 **IMAGE 2 – one card.** "×0.66 up to 1" under the machine, the STARVED bar along the bottom and the input it is short of lit up.

**Stuck loops.** Machines waiting on each other get a notice. Show me lights them up and the wires between them.

**EU per item.** The rate key has a fifth setting called EU. Each port then shows the EU its machine spends per item or litre. The overview shows what the whole plan spends on each of its inputs and outputs. Rates you set stay in your time unit. Hovering a port shows it too.

## Better

- Zoomed out cards kick in sooner. They show how hard each machine runs and their count is big enough to read.
- The overview lists every machine and every open input and output even at zero. A shared machine is one line there. You can change tier and amps from there too.
- Middle click clears any count or rate you set, on the card or in the overview.
- Tier and amps chips look like buttons again. Amps and machine counts are typed right where they show.
- The circuit shows in the corner of the machine picture instead of as an input.
- Deleting a drawer puts its rate back on the machine. Deleting one between two machines wires them together.
- The top bar is Plans, Share and your account. Invite people is the first thing in the Share menu and Community is a shelf in Plans.
- A pencil key on the board adds a sticky note, box or arrow. Peak and Avg moved to the overview.
- Each setting says what it does. Controls has the list of keys and the tour. You can turn the coloured outlines off there too.

## Fixed

- The "Nothing to calculate yet" line no longer sits over a task board.
- Old Build plans from links and codes turned their targets into rates. They read like they did in Build again.
- Fluid tanks from plan links and codes ran on their own and pushed 20 cells a second through the plan.

## Install

GTNH 2.9 only. Put `gtnhplanner-0.2.0-beta.3.jar` in your `mods` folder and delete the old one. It's client only.

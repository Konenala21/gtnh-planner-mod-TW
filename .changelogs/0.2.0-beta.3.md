Beta 3. Works on every GTNH 2.9 build from beta-1 to RC-2. Back up your world before updating.

Found a bug? Hit the red bug key in the planner or post in [our thread](https://discord.com/channels/181078474394566657/1531402304530682036) on the GTNH Discord.

> 📷 **IMAGE 1 – hero.** A line of cards with Up to counts. A green FULL bar, a red BOTTLENECK and an amber STARVED with its short input lit.

## New

**Machine counts work like real machines.** Set how many machines you have. The bar under the card shows how hard they run and why they can't run harder. Pick Exactly on the count if you want the old way.

> 📷 **IMAGE 2 – one card.** "×0.66 up to 1" under the machine, the STARVED bar along the bottom and the input it is short of lit up.

**Extreme Entity Crusher.** Every mob gets its own card. Click the mob's name to pick a different one.

- The planner warns you when machines starve or clog each other in a loop.
- The rate key has a new EU setting that shows how much power each item costs.
- Double-click a wire to add a dot. Drag the dot to move the wire.
- There's a Crop Farm card in the non-recipe machines.
- Crafting recipes can run on the Auto Workbench.
- Plan screenshots can be opened again as plans.

## Better

- Zoomed out cards show how hard each machine runs.
- The overview lists every machine and you can drag it wider.
- The top bar always fits on one row.
- Middle-click clears any count or rate you set.

> 📷 **IMAGE 3 – zoomed out.** A few cards far out, each with its bar and its count.

## Fixed

- Signing in no longer hides the plans you made before.
- The Rock Breaker no longer asks you for lava.
- Tree Growth Simulator logs now connect to the Industrial Coke Oven.
- Old plans from links and codes open the way they used to.

## Install

GTNH 2.9 only. Put `gtnhplanner-0.2.0-beta.3.jar` in your `mods` folder and delete the old one. It's client only.

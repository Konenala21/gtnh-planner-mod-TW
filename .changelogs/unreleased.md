## New

- **Up to counts.** A machine count is now what you have. Machines run as hard as the line lets them and say why when they can't. Pick Exactly on the count to force it like before.

## Better

- Deleting a drawer puts its rate back on the machine it hung off. Deleting a drawer between machines wires them straight together when there is only one way to.

## Fixed

- The "Nothing to calculate yet" line no longer sits over a task board.
- Fluid tanks from plan links and codes no longer run on their own. They pushed 20 cells a second through the plan with nothing set.

## New

- **Your plans in your account.** Sign in and every plan is saved to your account as you edit. They open on any computer and offline too. Your world's plans move over once and stay in the world as well.
- Plans shared with you say who shared them. Edits from others show up as they make them. Undo only takes back your own.
- **Plan together.** Share a plan with other players from the Share menu. Players in your game show up first and you can find anyone else by name. They edit it with you live.
- In a shared plan you see everyone else's cursor with their name. What they have selected gets an outline in their colour. Their faces sit at the top next to Live.
- Tasks in a shared plan can go to anyone it is shared with.
- **Task boards.** Every plan now has a Tasks view with To do, Doing, Blocked and Done. Tasks have notes, a checklist and comments. Drag them between columns. The + menu makes a new task board.
- Right-click cards on the plan to make a task from them. It gets a checklist line for each machine with how many to build. Cards on a task show a dot in its colour.
- Boxes and arrows on your board. Right-click empty board and pick Add box or Add arrow. Right-click one to change its colour and style. Drag a box by its border and a corner to resize it. Drag an arrow by its line and its ends to reshape it. Boxes, arrows and zones from shared plans show up too.
- **Groups.** Select a few things and press Group or Ctrl+G to put a frame round them. Drag a card into a group and it lights up and takes it. Drag it out the same way. Collapse a group to a small card with its machines and what goes in and out. Right-click it to rename, recolour or ungroup. Plan links and codes with boards open with them as groups.
- Drag a plan tab to move it along the strip. The others slide aside, it saves where you drop it, Esc puts it back. Ctrl+Shift+Left or Right moves the open plan's tab too.
- Share has Open in website. It opens your plan in the browser. The website can send a plan back to your game too and the game asks before it opens it.

## Better

- Share to library is now Publish to library. It posts a copy for everyone and never shares your plan itself.
- Right-click a card or a drawer for its menu, the same one its key opens.
- Share has Copy plan link and Export plan file. A plan you already posted gets Update in library and Publish as new post.

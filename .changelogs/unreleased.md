## New

- Boxes and arrows on your board. Right-click empty board and pick Add box or Add arrow. Right-click one to change its colour and style. Drag a box by its border and a corner to resize it. Drag an arrow by its line and its ends to reshape it. Boxes, arrows and zones from shared plans show up too.
- **Groups.** Select a few things and press Group or Ctrl+G to put a frame round them. Drag a card into a group and it lights up and takes it. Drag it out the same way. Collapse a group to a small card with its machines and what goes in and out. Right-click it to rename, recolour or ungroup. Plan links and codes with boards open with them as groups.

## Better

- Right-click a card or a drawer for its menu, the same one its key opens.

## New

- Double-click a wire to pin a dot on it. Drag the dot and the wire follows. Double-click the dot to take it off. Dots
  come along with plan links and codes.
- Drag the edge of the overview to resize it. Double-click the edge to reset it.
- New board settings. Zoomed-out view sets the zoom where cards turn simple. Scroll changes values lets you turn off the wheel on numbers and chips so it only zooms.
- Crafting recipes can run on the Auto Workbench. It costs 2048 EU a craft and gets faster each tier up to EV. The crafting table is still the free choice.
- A plan screenshot now carries the plan inside it. Open plan file on the + tab opens those and your exported plans.
- **Extreme Entity Crusher cards.** Every mob on NEI's Mob Drops page gets a card that kills it. Click its name to swap the mob. The gear sets your weapon, Looting, infernals, the ritual and damaged gear.
- A Crop Farm card in Non-recipe. It starts empty and you pick the crop on it. A crop card can switch crops from its menu.

## Better

- Dead loops and clog locks are hard to miss now. Each card in one says so on its bar. The warning on top blinks in the loop's colour and comes right after a failed solve.
- The top bar always fits on one row. Tabs scroll first and then Plans, Share and Sign in shrink to icons.
- A narrow planner shrinks the overview and then folds it so the board keeps its toolbar on one row. It opens again when there is room.
- Steam machines in the overview show the steam they burn instead of EU.
- Task spots have a Copy key for their coordinates.
- Plan links and codes open in the rate unit they were shared in.

## Fixed

- A loop that still gets fed from outside is no longer called a dead loop.
- The Rock Breaker no longer asks you to supply lava. Its lava slot shows as free.

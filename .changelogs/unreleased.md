## New

- Drag the edge of the overview to resize it. Double-click the edge to reset it.
- New board settings. Zoomed-out view sets the zoom where cards turn simple. Scroll changes values lets you turn off the wheel on numbers and chips so it only zooms.

## Better

- The top bar always fits on one row. Tabs scroll first and then Plans, Share and Sign in shrink to icons.
- A narrow planner shrinks the overview and then folds it so the board keeps its toolbar on one row. It opens again when there is room.

## New

- **Up to counts.** A machine count is now what you have. Machines run as hard as the line lets them and say why when they can't. A card with a problem gets a coloured outline and the port holding it back lights up. You can turn the outlines off in settings. Pick Exactly on the count to force it like before.

## Better

- A recipe's circuit sits in the corner of its machine. The circuit setting is gone.
- Middle click clears a count or rate you set.
- Tier and amps chips look like buttons again. Amps are typed right into the chip.
- Zoomed out cards kick in sooner and show how hard each machine runs.
- Deleting a drawer puts its rate back on the machine it hung off. Deleting a drawer between machines wires them straight together when there is only one way to.

## Fixed

- The "Nothing to calculate yet" line no longer sits over a task board.
- Old Build plans from links and codes no longer turn their targets into rates. They read like they did in Build again.
- Fluid tanks from plan links and codes no longer run on their own. They pushed 20 cells a second through the plan with nothing set.

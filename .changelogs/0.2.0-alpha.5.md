Fifth alpha of GTNH Planner: plan GT New Horizons factories in game, from NEI.

**This is an alpha.** Expect rough edges, and keep a backup of your world's planner saves (`gtnhplanner/plans.dat`, backed up automatically to `backups/` beside it). Bugs and ideas: the feedback key on the planner's top bar, or GTNH Planner's thread on the GT New Horizons Discord (the Discord key).

**For every GTNH 2.9 build.** One jar runs on all of them, from 2.9.0-beta-1 to 2.9.0-RC-2 (it is made against RC-2's mods: NEI 2.8.155, ModularUI2 2.3.92, GTNHLib 0.11.52, GT5-Unofficial 5.09.54, CropsNH 2.0.134). It is not for GTNH 2.8; stay on 0.2.0-alpha.1 there.

## New

- **Pin list**: pin a plan's machines to the screen while you play, to gather what a build needs. A card's pin key, or the new pin key beside each machine in the overview, offers Pin to the screen; the list shows each pinned machine with its tier and count in a corner. Right-click a pin key to unpin. The gear's Pin list section shows or hides it and picks its corner, and a key to toggle it can be bound in Controls.
- **The planner's own size**: the planner now sits a GUI scale step smaller than NEI's list beside it, so it is not too big at GUI scale 3 while NEI keeps its size. Pick Tiny, Small, Normal or Large under Size in the gear's settings; Large is NEI's own size, as before. Normal never goes below GUI scale 2.

## Changed

- The mouse wheel over the overview's rates scrolls the overview instead of changing them.

## Fixed

- On GTNH 2.9.0-RC-1 and the betas, opening a recipe page in NEI no longer crashes the game. The planner's button is there again, above NEI's own.
- On GTNH 2.9.0-beta-1 and beta-2, GregTech recipes can be added to a plan again (the Tree Growth Simulator, which those builds lack, is simply not worked out there).
- A mod whose recipes the planner cannot read in your pack no longer stops the game from starting; only that mod's recipes are left out (on 2.9.0-beta-1, Thaumic Tinkerer's necromancy).

## Upgrading

At GUI scale 3 or more the planner opens a step smaller than before (at 2 or less it is as it was). To get the old size back, choose Large under Size in the gear's settings.

## Install

1. Drop `gtnhplanner-0.2.0-alpha.5.jar` into your GTNH 2.9 instance's `mods` folder, replacing any older `gtnhplanner` jar (client only; servers don't need it).
2. Open your inventory: the GTNH Planner button is at the bottom left.

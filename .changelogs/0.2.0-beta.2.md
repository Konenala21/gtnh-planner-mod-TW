Beta 2. You can plan together now. Sign in and your plans save to your account as you edit. Share one and you work on it together live, in game or in a browser. Plans also got task boards, groups and drawings. It's still a beta so the planner keeps a copy of your world's plans.

Found a bug? Hit the red bug key in the planner or post in [our thread](https://discord.com/channels/181078474394566657/1531402304530682036) on the GTNH Discord.

## New

**Plan together.** Open Share and pick Share with people. Players in your game show up first. You can find anyone else by name. You see their cursor and what they have selected. Their edits show up as they make them. Undo only takes back your own.

> 📷 **IMAGE 2 – Share with people.** The box with In this game and a name typed in the search.

**Your plans in your account.** Sign in from the Library and every plan saves to your account as you edit. They open on any computer and in a browser too. Your world's plans move over once and stay in the world as well. Signed out nothing changes.

**Task boards.** Every plan has a Tasks view with To do, Doing, Blocked and Done. A task has notes, a checklist, comments and the people on it. Right-click some cards to make a task from them with a line for each machine and how many to build. New task board in the + menu makes a plan that opens on its tasks.

> 📷 **IMAGE 3 – Tasks.** The four columns with a few tasks. One with faces and a checklist.

**Groups.** Select some cards and press Ctrl+G. Drag cards in and out. Collapse a group to a small card that shows what goes in and out.

> 📷 **IMAGE 4 – Groups.** One group open and one collapsed next to it.

- Boxes and arrows. Right-click empty board and pick Add box or Add arrow.
- Buffer drawers. Drop a card's input on a product drawer and it sits between the cards that make the item and the ones that use it.
- Drag plan tabs to put them in order.
- Share has Open in website, Copy plan link and Export plan file.

## Better

- Share to library is now Publish to library. It posts a copy for everyone and never shares your plan itself.
- Right-click a card or a drawer for its menu.
- Plans from links, codes and the library keep their layout. Cards no longer land on top of each other.

## Fixed

- Plans from the library, links and codes lost machine options like coils and pipe casings. Their numbers come out right now.
- Trash drawers went to the library as product drawers.

## Install

GTNH 2.9 only: any beta or release candidate. It does not run on 2.8. Stay on 0.2.0-alpha.1 there.

Put `gtnhplanner-0.2.0-beta.2.jar` in your `mods` folder and delete the old one. It's client only. The planner button is at the bottom left of your inventory. To plan with others sign in from the Library.

Curious how it works inside? The [technical notes](0.2.0-beta.2-technical.md) go through how a plan stays in sync between players with links into the code.
